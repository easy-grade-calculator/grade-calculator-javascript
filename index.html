JavaScript Grade Calculator

Enter the points you earned and the total possible points to calculate your percentage and letter grade.

How the Grade Calculator Works

The calculator divides the points earned by the total possible points and multiplies the result by 100.

Formula: Grade Percentage = (Points Earned ÷ Total Points) × 100

For a more complete calculator with additional grading tools, visit Easy Grade Calculator .

Example

If a student earns 85 points out of 100 possible points, the grade percentage is 85%, which is normally considered a B grade.
